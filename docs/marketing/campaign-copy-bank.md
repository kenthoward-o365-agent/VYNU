## 6. Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "We already have me&u / Mr Yum." | Those are digital menus with a checkout. We replace the menu with a conversation and attribute the revenue it adds. Migration is a weekend and the stickers are ours to print. |
| "Our staff won't adopt it." | Nothing changes on the floor except fewer order-takers. Orders land in the POS you already use, with kitchen pacing and throttling so the pass doesn't get buried. |
| "AI will get orders wrong." | Guardrails are venue-set: your menu, your modifiers, your allergen data, your discount ceiling. The AI can't offer what you haven't loaded. |
| "What about card surcharges?" | H&L Pay is AU-surcharging compliant, configurable per venue, with weekend and special-date rules for public holidays and events. |
| "Who holds the money?" | We do — built-in PayFac, one statement, no third-party processor handoff, PCI DSS SAQ-A scope. |
| "We're not on H&L POS." | It runs fully standalone. If you move to H&L POS later, it's a native integration, not a rebuild. |
